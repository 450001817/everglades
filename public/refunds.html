Refund Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

In short: Eco-bucks are delivered instantly and purchases are final. If something goes wrong (you were charged but didn't receive your Eco-bucks, you were charged twice, or you didn't make the purchase), contact us within 30 days and we'll make it right.

What you're buying

Eco-bucks (EB) are an in-game currency for Everglades. You can buy them in these packs: 500 EB for $5.00, 1,000 EB for $9.00, and 2,000 EB for $15.00 (US dollars). Payments are processed by Stripe. Eco-bucks are added to your account as soon as your payment is confirmed. They have no cash value outside the game.

All sales are final

Because Eco-bucks are digital and delivered right away, purchases are generally non-refundable. This includes Eco-bucks you have already spent, and items you bought with Eco-bucks in the in-game store (skins, chat lines, foil cards and titles). Eco-bucks can't be exchanged back for money.

When we will refund you

We will refund a purchase, to the payment method you used, if you contact us within 30 days of the charge and:

  • You didn't receive your Eco-bucks. You were charged, but the Eco-bucks never appeared in your account, and we can't add them for you.
  • You were charged more than once for the same purchase, or charged the wrong amount.
  • You didn't make the purchase. Someone used your payment method or account without your permission. We may ask for details to look into it.
  • The law requires it where you live.

We may also refund other purchases at our discretion, for example if you accidentally bought the wrong pack and haven't spent any of the Eco-bucks.

What happens when we refund

We remove the refunded Eco-bucks from your account. If you've already spent some of them, your balance may go below zero until you earn or buy more. Stripe usually returns refunds to your card within 5 to 10 business days, depending on your bank.

Chargebacks

Please contact us before disputing a charge with your bank. We can usually fix problems faster. If a payment is disputed, we may remove the Eco-bucks it bought and pause the account while the dispute is open.

How to ask for a refund

Contact us at the support contact shown on your Stripe receipt email. Include your gamertag, the date of the purchase, and the receipt or the last 4 digits of the card you used. We aim to reply within 3 business days.
